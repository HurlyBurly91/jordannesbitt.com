# M07 — Professional site and acquisition path

## Objective
Connect the body of work, professional context and a clear enquiry path without intrusive sales presentation.

## Scope
Image-led homepage; concise About and separate CV; optional genuine Studio/Journal content; Available view; honest artwork-level price/edition/framing/status rendering; contextual enquiry links/form adapter and accessible contact fallback. Generate all views from the canonical content rather than duplicating listings.

## Non-goals
Real message sends, checkout, invented biography/awards/terms/prices, compulsory newsletter registration or automatic public deployment.

## Domain-state dependencies
READ: docs/design.md, docs/acquisition.md, docs/catalogue.md.
MAY MODIFY: docs/acquisition.md for tested enquiry behavior.
MUST PRESERVE: docs/operations.md external-effect and hosting gates.

## Acceptance criteria
- Fixture browser journey: discover project -> inspect work -> understand availability -> initiate a correctly addressed, work-identified enquiry.
- Tests cover available/sold/enquiry-only/unknown, absent prices and missing recipient without fake success or broken calls to action.
- No unsupported CV facts or construction copy can pass the later release check.
- Draft policies and real delivery checks remain explicitly gated for M09/M10, not claimed complete.

## Gate
Technical capability acceptance; factual content and recipient/offer approval are M09. Next: M08.
